# Overworld — Dialogues

## Presentation

- Use the standard retro-inspired dialogue and narration box at the bottom of the screen.
- Character animations should play before dialogue appears when applicable.
- Internal monologues are presented as text, without spoken dialogue.
- Keep internal monologues very short, usually one sentence.
- Avoid excessive narration.

## OVERWORLD_INTRO

**Trigger:** Beginning of the game.

**Presentation:** One representative image of the interconnected multiverse remains visible throughout the introduction.

**Narrator:**

"Beyond the sky lies a multiverse of interconnected dream worlds."

"Each world represents a day of the week, shaped by its own element. Together, they form the calendar."

"But there was one who despised the very beginning of each week..."

"The Grand Wizard, a frog who absolutely hated Mondays."

"One day, he discovered a technique in an ancient historical book. It claimed that swallowing the Moon would erase Monday from the calendar."

"Naturally, he decided to try it."

"The Moon disappeared into his stomach, and the calendar collapsed. The seven worlds fell out of balance."

"But the ancient book also revealed a way to restore the week."

"Gather the Sun and Moon Essences, combine them into a magical soup, and feed it to the one who swallowed the Moon."

"And so, someone would have to journey through the dream worlds to restore the calendar."

Transition: The introduction fades into the Overworld. The player gains control after the opening presentation.

**Player:**

"Wait... no Monday means no Cyber Monday?!"

"Now I can't buy a washing machine on discount!!!"

"I need to wash my blanket without it..."

"..."

"I need to get Monday back."

## OVERWORLD_FIRE_RETURN

**Trigger:** Player returns from Fire.

**Presentation:** Internal monologue.

**Protagonist:**

"I have a Candle... now I need something to cook in."

## OVERWORLD_EARTH_RETURN

**Trigger:** Player returns from Earth.

**Presentation:** Internal monologue.

**Protagonist:**

"Now I need some water to fill this pot."

## OVERWORLD_WATER_RETURN

**Trigger:** Player places the filled pot onto the cooking station.

**Presentation:** Internal monologue.

**Protagonist:**

"Now I need some firewood to get this going."

## OVERWORLD_WOOD_RETURN

**Trigger:** Player successfully lights the fire beneath the cooking pot.

**Presentation:** Internal monologue.

**Protagonist:**

"The fire's ready. Now I just need something to stir it with."

## OVERWORLD_METAL_RETURN

**Trigger:** Player returns from Metal with the Sword.

**Presentation:** Internal monologue.

**Protagonist:**

"A sword... well, I suppose that'll work."

## OVERWORLD_SUN_RETURN

**Trigger:** Player obtains Sun Essence during the Sun encounter.

**Presentation:** Internal monologue or brief thought during the continuous Sun-to-Moon sequence.

**Protagonist:**

"One down. One more to go."

## OVERWORLD_MOON_RETURN

**Trigger:** Player returns to the Overworld with Moon Essence and the Frog Wizard.

**Presentation:** Internal monologue.

**Protagonist:**

"That's everything. Time to finish the soup."

## OVERWORLD_ENDING

**Trigger:** Player feeds the finished soup to the Frog Wizard.

**Presentation:** One representative ending image. The exact image composition is tentative.

**Frog Wizard:**

"NOOO! MONDAY'S BACK!"

**Protagonist:**

"Well... at least the week is whole again."

## Notes

- The introduction uses one image of the multiverse, with narration appearing sequentially.
- The ending uses one image showing the Moon's return.
- The Frog Wizard's reaction should be comedic.
- Internal monologues should be brief and guide the player towards the next objective.
- The Sun and Moon encounters are continuous, so the Sun monologue should not trigger a return to the Overworld.
- The ending concludes immediately after the final exchange. No additional epilogue or gameplay.
