# Overworld — Scenery

## Overview

A small, peaceful floating island suspended above an endless sea of clouds.

The island is home to a cozy little cottage and serves as the central hub of Dreamweek. The player returns here between dreams to gather materials and gradually prepare the magical soup.

The environment should feel like a peaceful sanctuary floating somewhere between reality and dreams.

The visual direction is highly stylized, dreamy, and atmospheric, with smooth organic shapes, beautiful colour gradients, soft lighting, and subtle magical details.

Blue is the dominant colour throughout the environment, complemented by accents of pink, purple, and magenta.

## The Floating Island

### Main Island

One small floating island forms the entire playable area.

- A soft, organic silhouette rather than a perfectly circular platform.
- A lush grassy surface with gentle elevation changes.
- Uneven edges with exposed rock formations underneath.
- A few roots and small plants hanging beneath the island.
- A handful of smaller floating rocks surrounding the island.

The island should feel natural and slightly magical, as though it has always been floating here.

The playable surface should remain relatively open, with enough room for the cottage, hammock, cooking station, and player movement.

### Floating Details

A few smaller floating rocks and fragments surround the main island.

- Small rocks at different heights and distances.
- Occasional tiny patches of vegetation.
- Distant floating land silhouettes.
- Subtle drifting particles around the island.

These details should establish the feeling of a larger floating world without creating additional explorable areas.

## The Cottage

A small, cozy cottage sits on the island and acts as its main architectural feature.

- Whimsical storybook-inspired architecture.
- Soft blue exterior with subtle lighter and darker blue details.
- A charming sloped roof with an organic silhouette.
- Small windows with warm interior light.
- A simple wooden door.
- A few flowers, bushes, and vegetation around the exterior.

The cottage should feel welcoming and slightly magical, as though it belongs naturally on this floating island.

The player does not enter the cottage. There is no interior to explore.

## The Sky and Clouds

The sky is one of the most important parts of the Overworld's visual identity.

### Sky

A beautiful pre-sunset sky dominated by shades of blue.

- Soft sky blue as the primary colour.
- Deeper blue and cyan in some areas.
- Gentle gradients transitioning into pink, purple, and magenta.
- Subtle lavender and peach tones near the horizon.

Blue should remain visually dominant. Pink, purple, and magenta should complement it rather than overwhelm it.

The overall atmosphere should feel like the quiet moments before sunset: peaceful, dreamy, and slightly magical.

The Moon is initially absent because it has been swallowed by the Frog Wizard.

### Clouds

An endless sea of soft clouds surrounds the island.

- Large, soft cloud formations beneath the island.
- Smaller wisps drifting around its edges.
- Multiple layers of clouds to create depth.
- Gentle movement to make the world feel alive.

Clouds should primarily appear white, pale blue, and soft cyan, with subtle pink, purple, and magenta reflections.

## Main Features

### 1. The Hammock

A simple hammock is placed outside the cottage in a peaceful corner of the island.

It serves as the entrance to the dream worlds.

- Suspended between two trees or simple wooden supports.
- Soft fabric with slightly curved edges.
- Positioned somewhere scenic, overlooking the clouds.
- A comfortable and inviting appearance.

The player interacts with the hammock to lie down and enter the next dream.

There is no need for a house interior or separate sleeping area.

### 2. Cooking Station

The cooking station is placed in an open clearing near the centre of the island.

It is the main progression feature of the Overworld and gradually becomes more complete as the player collects materials.

The station consists of:

- A simple circular stone hearth.
- A raised metal support for holding the soup pot.
- An open space beneath the pot for placing firewood.
- Enough surrounding space for the player to interact with the setup.

Initially, the cooking station is empty.

As the player progresses:
- The filled pot is manually placed onto the cooking station after the Water dream.
- Firewood is placed beneath it after the Wood dream.
- The Candle is used to light the fire.
- The Sword is used to stir the soup.
- Sun and Moon essence are added towards the end.

The cooking station should feel naturally integrated into the island rather than like a modern appliance.

## Vegetation and Small Details

Keep the island lush but not overly crowded.

Possible details:

- A few small trees with soft, rounded foliage.
- Wildflowers scattered across the grass.
- Small mushrooms and bushes.
- A few unusual glowing plants.
- Floating magical particles.
- Occasional butterflies or tiny glowing creatures.

Vegetation should use organic shapes and harmonious colours.

Avoid excessive environmental props. The island should remain open and visually calm.

## Lighting and Atmosphere

Lighting is essential to the dreamy appearance.

- Soft ambient illumination across the island.
- Cool blue illumination from the sky.
- Subtle pink, purple, and magenta reflections.
- Gentle warm highlights on the cottage and vegetation.
- Soft shadows without harsh contrast.
- Atmospheric haze around distant clouds and floating objects.

The environment should feel luminous without everything glowing.

The cooking station can introduce an additional warm light source once the fire is lit.

## Colour Palette

| Element | Colours |
|---|---|
| Sky | Primarily blue, with pink, purple, and magenta gradients |
| Clouds | White, pale blue, cyan, subtle pink and lavender |
| Cottage | Soft blue, darker blue accents, warm window light |
| Grass | Soft green, muted teal |
| Rocks | Blue-grey, muted purple |
| Vegetation | Natural green with occasional pastel flowers |
| Magical details | Subtle cyan, pink, and white |
| Cooking fire | Warm amber and orange |

The palette should feel harmonious, with blue remaining the dominant visual identity.

## Visual Style

The Overworld should establish the visual quality of Dreamweek.

- Smooth, rounded, organic shapes.
- Stylized 3D scenery with beautiful colour gradients.
- Soft atmospheric lighting.
- Dreamy cloud formations and layered backgrounds.
- Gentle emissive details and floating particles.
- A slightly surreal sense of scale and space.

Avoid realistic architecture, excessive low-poly geometry, hard industrial shapes, or cluttered environments.

The visual direction should feel whimsical and mysterious, with a polished real-time 3D game aesthetic.

## Environmental Progression

The Overworld gradually changes as the player progresses through the dreams.

- The cooking station becomes more complete as materials are collected.
- The fire introduces a warm glow into the clearing.
- The completed soup creates a magical focal point.
- At the ending, the actual Moon returns to the sky.

The changes should feel meaningful but should not require transforming the entire environment.

## Visual Priorities

1. The floating island and surrounding sea of clouds.
2. The blue-dominant pre-sunset sky.
3. The cozy cottage.
4. The hammock as the dream entrance.
5. The central cooking station.
6. The surrounding vegetation and magical details.

## Scope and Constraints

- One small floating island.
- One exterior cottage; no interior or explorable buildings.
- The hammock is the dream entrance.
- The cooking station is the central progression point.
- No additional islands that require exploration.
- Keep environmental details decorative rather than interactive.
- Use atmosphere, lighting, colour, and composition to create visual richness rather than adding lots of assets.

## Production specification

**Essential.** From the wake point, frame the hearth centre foreground, cottage back left and hammock at the right cloud edge. Fit them inside a walkable 18 × 14 unit oval. Displace 4 concentric rings with the same low-frequency outline to form grass lip, rocky overhang and tapered underside. Keep the top gently sloped and the last 0.6 units inside the lip walkable only up to a safe edge. Give the 4-unit-wide cottage a curved blue gable, deep eave, recessed arched wooden door, two amber windows and an exterior sill. Hang a 2.2-unit cloth strip between posts with a parabolic sag; lower its middle under the sleeping spirit. Place stone hearth ring and iron trivet 3 units clear of the cottage. Show the same carried pot profile on it, logs beneath, flame, small boil bubbles, gold and lavender swirls, Sword stir and a ladled bowl in sequence.

Sky bands from #367DD1 overhead to #9BCFEA at eye level; localise pink #EAB0CE and magenta #A66BAD on one side. Put a broad pale-blue cloud deck well below the island, plus a few smooth opaque banks at different depths. Light the cottage from the window side, keep pink fill off the blue roof. The Moon appears in the ending plate.

**Secondary polish.** Sweep short grass in patches around paths, expose trailing roots along the cliff, subtly drift a few cloud banks. A warm reflected patch grows on the stones after ignition. **Optional.** Two distant fragments and sparse wildflowers. Study [island layering](references/scenary/floating_island.jpeg).
