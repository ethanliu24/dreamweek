# Metal — Scenery

## Overview

An ancient magical forge hidden deep underground.

The environment is a mysterious cavern that feels like an old sacred place where metalworking and magic were once practiced together.

The visual direction should be fantastical, atmospheric, and slightly mysterious. Avoid steampunk or industrial factory aesthetics.

## Environment

### Underground Cavern

A large natural cavern forms the main environment.

- High cave ceilings and dark stone walls.
- Large rock formations and occasional stalactites.
- Uneven stone floors with a few natural elevation changes.
- Ancient stone structures built into the cavern walls.
- Subtle engraved patterns and runes on selected surfaces.

The cavern should feel spacious, but gameplay should take place in one relatively compact area.

### Ancient Forge

The forge is the main visual landmark.

- A large ancient structure made from dark stone and aged metal.
- A central furnace or crucible with a warm magical glow.
- Simple architectural details and engraved symbols.
- Warm light illuminating the surrounding stone.
- A small display stand nearby holding the Sword.

The forge should feel almost ceremonial, rather than like an ordinary blacksmith's workshop.

Initially, the forge is inactive and its magical glow is dim. After the pipe puzzle is completed, it activates and illuminates the chamber.

### Magical Pipe Network

A collection of old metal pipes runs through the chamber and connects to the forge.

- Pipes follow the stone walls and extend along parts of the ceiling.
- Use aged copper, bronze, and dark metal materials.
- Selected sections contain visible glowing magical liquid.
- Magical energy can be seen flowing through active sections.

Most pipes are environmental decoration. The main puzzle is a specific section of the network mounted directly onto a prominent stone wall near the forge.

#### Pipe Puzzle Wall

- A flat, clearly visible stone wall section.
- Contains a fixed 10-row × 10-column grid of pipe pieces.
- The grid is integrated into the ancient architecture.
- The starting point and endpoint are visually distinguishable.
- Pipe pieces should be large enough to see and interact with comfortably.
- The entire grid should be visible from a front-facing camera position.

The puzzle wall should look like an old magical mechanism rather than a modern control panel.

When the puzzle is solved, glowing energy travels through the connected pipes and continues towards the forge.

## Magical Details

Keep magical elements subtle and concentrated around the forge and pipe network.

- Glowing runes engraved into stone and metal.
- Soft magical particles floating near the forge.
- Faint light emerging from cracks in the cavern.
- Glowing liquid flowing through active sections of the pipes.
- Occasional crystals embedded in the cave walls.

Avoid filling the entire environment with glowing objects. The magical details should feel special.

## Lighting and Colour Palette

The environment should contrast dark surroundings with magical light.

- **Base:** Deep charcoal, dark blue-grey, and muted stone colours.
- **Metal:** Aged bronze, copper, and dark steel.
- **Magical glow:** Teal or cyan.
- **Forge light:** Warm amber and orange.

The warm forge light should contrast with the cooler magical glow.

Keep the area sufficiently illuminated for gameplay and navigation.

## Visual Priorities

1. The ancient forge as the main landmark.
2. The wall-mounted pipe puzzle.
3. The larger decorative pipe network.
4. The Sword and its display stand.
5. The cavern architecture and small magical details.

## Scope and Constraints

- Use one main cavern chamber.
- Avoid creating multiple underground rooms or an elaborate dungeon.
- The forge should be the main visual focal point.
- Keep the playable pipe puzzle to one 10 × 10 grid.
- Larger pipes are environmental decoration, not additional puzzles.
- Avoid steampunk elements such as excessive gears, machinery, gauges, and industrial equipment.
- Magical effects should be simple and atmospheric rather than complicated simulations.
- Spend the most visual detail on the forge and its immediate surroundings.

## Production specification

**Essential.** Place a stepped dark-stone forge at the end of a compact chamber under a high ribbed cavern roof. Put Clink and the Sword plinth in front of the furnace, puzzle on the adjoining wall angled into the arrival view. A 5-unit-wide inset holds ten columns and ten rows; each cell is square. Straight pieces use tubes, elbows use quarter-annulus sweeps, each with two matching collars. Empty recesses are matte. Raised source has a pulsing droplet and sink a flared mouth. Decorative trunk pipes enter and exit at those sockets. Puzzle shot square to the wall with ≥10% margin on every side. Use #303746 stone, #91634F aged copper, #54DBCB flow stripe. On solution a single front of light follows the actual connected path outward to the furnace. The crucible then changes from dim to amber and lights the Sword stand. Sword has a tapered silver blade 0.85 units long, small curved guard and dark grip; keep its slim outline legible against a matte backdrop.

**Secondary polish.** A broad cool highlight curves around bronze pipe collars, engravings catch furnace light as it wakes. **Optional.** A few high stalactites and scattered sparks confined to the crucible.
