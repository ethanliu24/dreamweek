# Metal — Story

## Overview

The player enters an ancient underground forge and catches the attention of Clink, a small but bossy beetle guardian.

The forge has stopped working because its magical pipe network is broken. Clink recruits the player to help reconnect the pipes, promising a Sword as a reward.

After the player repairs the pipes, Clink fulfils his promise and gives them the Sword.

**Target duration:** 2–3 minutes.

## Story Scenes

### Scene 1 — Arrival

**Scene ID:** `METAL_INTRO`

**Presentation:** Brief establishing shot followed by character interaction.

The player spawns inside the underground chamber. The ancient forge and its magical pipe network are visible.

Clink notices the player from a distance and immediately calls out to them, asking for help and promising a Sword as a reward.

**Camera:**
- Begin with a wide shot establishing the cavern and ancient forge.
- Show Clink noticing the player.
- Move the camera towards Clink as he calls out.

**Character animation:**
- Clink notices the player.
- He raises his body and waves his front legs to get their attention.
- He gestures impatiently for the player to come over.

**Player control:** Disabled during the initial presentation and interaction. Enabled afterwards so the player can approach Clink.

**Dialogue reference:** `METAL_INTRO`

### Scene 2 — The Problem

**Scene ID:** `METAL_EXPLANATION`

**Presentation:** Short character interaction.

Once the player approaches, Clink explains that the magical pipe network has broken, interrupting the flow of energy throughout the forge.

He points towards the pipes and tells the player to reconnect them.

**Camera:**
- Frame Clink and the player together.
- Briefly show the broken pipe network.
- Return to Clink as he gestures towards the problem.

**Character animation:**
- Clink points emphatically towards the pipes.
- He gestures along the direction of the broken connection.
- He finishes by impatiently directing the player towards the puzzle.

**Player control:** Disabled during the interaction, then enabled for gameplay.

**Dialogue reference:** `METAL_EXPLANATION`

### Scene 3 — Repair the Pipes

**Scene ID:** `METAL_PUZZLE`

**Presentation:** Gameplay.

The player completes the pipe-connecting puzzle.

Once the pipes are correctly connected, magical energy begins flowing through the network again. The ancient forge activates and lights up.

**Camera:**
- Keep the puzzle area clearly visible.
- Once completed, briefly show magical energy flowing through the pipes.
- Show the forge lighting up.

**Character animation:**
- Clink watches the player work.
- As the pipes activate, his antennae rise and he becomes visibly excited.

**Player control:** Enabled during the puzzle. Temporarily disabled during the completion sequence.

### Scene 4 — The Reward

**Scene ID:** `METAL_REWARD`

**Presentation:** Short character interaction.

Clink celebrates that the forge is working again and fulfils his promise by presenting the Sword.

The player explains that they are gathering things to brew a special soup. Clink realizes the Sword could be useful for stirring it.

**Camera:**
- Show Clink proudly presenting the Sword.
- Briefly focus on the Sword as the player receives it.
- Return to a medium shot of Clink and the player.

**Character animation:**
- Clink stands proudly after the forge activates.
- He gestures towards the Sword.
- He briefly looks puzzled when the player mentions the soup, then responds confidently.

**Player control:** Disabled during the interaction. Restored after receiving the Sword.

**Dialogue reference:** `METAL_REWARD`

### Scene 5 — Return

**Scene ID:** `METAL_EXIT`

**Presentation:** Transition.

The player leaves the underground forge and returns to the overworld.

**Camera:** Fade to black, then transition to the overworld.

**Player control:** Disabled during the transition.

## Storyboard Summary

1. Establish the underground cavern and ancient forge.
2. Clink notices the player and calls them over.
3. Clink promises a Sword in exchange for help.
4. The player approaches Clink and learns about the broken pipes.
5. The player completes the pipe puzzle.
6. Magical energy flows through the pipes and the forge activates.
7. Clink gives the player the promised Sword.
8. The player explains the soup objective.
9. Clink comments on the Sword being useful for stirring.
10. Fade back to the overworld.

## Notes

- Clink should be energetic, loud, confident, and commanding.
- His initial interaction should establish his personality immediately.
- The player should first hear Clink's request from a distance, then approach him for the explanation.
- Keep the explanation of the pipe problem brief.
- The Sword is an old artifact kept within the ancient forge.
- The player receives the Sword as both a weapon for later arena encounters and a tool for stirring the soup.
- Keep the environment to one main underground chamber.
- Do not introduce additional characters, side quests, or unnecessary interactions.
