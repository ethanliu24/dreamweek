# Metal — Dialogues

## Presentation

- Use the standard retro-inspired dialogue box at the bottom of the screen.
- Character animations should play before their dialogue appears.
- Clink speaks loudly, confidently, and directly.
- His dialogue should feel commanding and slightly impatient.
- Keep the exchanges short.

## METAL_INTRO

**Trigger:** Player spawns in the underground chamber. Clink notices them from a distance.

**Clink:**

"HEY! You there!"

"Come help me and I'll give you a SWORD!"

"Now, come over here!"

## METAL_EXPLANATION

**Trigger:** Player approaches Clink.

**Clink:**

"See this piping system? It's completely broken!"

"The magical flow has stopped!"

"I need you to reconnect these pipes."

"Simple enough, right? Let's get moving!"

## METAL_REWARD

**Trigger:** Player completes the pipe puzzle and interacts with Clink.

**Clink:**

"Ha! There we go! Working perfectly!"

"A promise is a promise. Here's your sword!"

## Notes

- Clink should sound like he's used to giving orders.
- His initial request should be loud and attention-grabbing.
- Keep a small pause before he reveals the Sword reward.
- Clink should be genuinely pleased when the forge works again.
- The player only explains the soup objective after receiving the Sword.
