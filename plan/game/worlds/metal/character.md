# Clink — Metal Guardian

## Overview

Clink is a small beetle guardian who looks after the ancient underground forge and its magical pipe network.

He is confident, bossy, impatient, and very direct. He takes his responsibilities seriously, but has no problem ordering a random visitor to help him when something goes wrong.

Despite being a tiny creature, Clink acts as though he is in charge of the entire underground facility.

## Appearance

Clink has a rounded beetle-like appearance with naturally metallic features.

- **Body:** Small, rounded body with a dark bronze metallic finish.
- **Shell:** Large domed copper-bronze shell with a few engraved magical patterns.
- **Face:** Two large glowing teal eyes and a small mouth.
- **Antennae:** Two short antennae with tiny glowing tips.
- **Legs:** Six short, stubby legs.
- **Details:** Subtle turquoise markings across his shell, resembling traces of magical energy.

His shell should be his defining feature.

His design should feel like a living magical creature, not a mechanical robot. Avoid excessive armor, gears, or industrial details.

## Colour Palette

- Dark bronze and aged copper for the body and shell.
- Warm gold highlights.
- Teal or cyan for the eyes and magical markings.

His metallic colours should complement the underground forge environment.

## Personality

Clink is confident, bossy, impatient, and commanding.

- Speaks loudly and directly.
- Has no hesitation in asking others for help.
- Often gives instructions rather than politely requesting things.
- Doesn't like waiting around.
- Is extremely confident, sometimes overestimating his authority.
- Makes generous promises when he needs something done.
- Gets excited and pleased when things go his way.

Despite his demanding nature, Clink is not malicious. He genuinely appreciates help and keeps his promises.

The comedy comes from how seriously he takes his role and how confidently he orders the player around, despite being a tiny beetle.

## Behaviour and Expressions

- **Idle:** Stands confidently, occasionally moving his antennae while observing the forge.
- **Calling:** Raises his body slightly and waves his front legs to get the player's attention.
- **Explaining:** Points emphatically towards the broken pipe network.
- **Impatient:** Taps his feet and moves his antennae rapidly.
- **Excited:** Raises his antennae and gestures enthusiastically.
- **Pleased:** Stands proudly, satisfied that the forge is working again.

## Role in the Story

Clink is the caretaker of the ancient underground forge. He looks after the magical pipe network that carries energy throughout the chamber.

When the player arrives, the forge is not working because the magical flow through its pipes has been interrupted.

Clink immediately calls the player over and offers them a Sword in exchange for helping him repair the pipes.

After the player restores the forge, Clink fulfils his promise and gives them the Sword.

## Character Direction

Clink should feel like a tiny, overly confident foreman who believes he is in charge of everything.

His personality should be energetic and expressive, contrasting with his small size and the enormous ancient forge he maintains.

## Production specification

**Essential.** Clink stands 0.55 units tall, shell length 0.75. Sweep a single egg-shaped copper carapace around a darker belly with a fine central seam. Half-bury six short tapered legs, two glowing eye domes and two curved short antennae with luminous tips. A small lower mouth opens beneath the eyes. Bronze #795841 shell, golden edges, dark underside, teal #5FDDCE eyes and two incised-looking lines. Set roughness ~0.42 to catch the forge's broad key. Put him on the stone step high enough to see from spawn. Raise the whole front while calling, point one foreleg toward the wall, tap impatiently, splay antennae and lift the Sword on success.

**Secondary polish.** Slightly different patina on seam and tips; alternate front-leg tap while pipe turns. **Optional.** One shallow rune on each wing case. Study [beetle shell silhouette](references/clink/clink_1.jpeg).
