# Fire — Dialogue

## Presentation

Dialogue is displayed in a simple, retro-inspired text box at the bottom of the screen.

- Character animations play first to communicate the action and emotion.
- The dialogue box appears after the animation beat.
- Display the character's name and a short line of text.
- Keep dialogue to one or two lines at most.
- The player advances dialogue through a button press or click.
- Characters hold their expression or a subtle idle animation while dialogue is displayed.
- No voice acting or detailed lip-sync is required.

## Dialogue Direction

Wick takes himself very seriously despite being an inherently ridiculous character.

His dialogue should be:
- Silly and playful.
- Short and easy to read.
- Occasionally dramatic.
- Consistent with his overly confident personality.

Use animation and visual comedy wherever possible instead of adding more dialogue.

---

## FIRE_INTRO

**Trigger:** After Wick's lantern rises above the festival.

**Animation:** Wick proudly gestures towards the festival.

**Wick:**
"My first festival! Isn't it magnificent?!"

---

## FIRE_SEAGULL

**Trigger:** Wick notices the seagull.

**Animation:** Wick follows the bird from right to left, then follows its falling poop downwards. He looks down and realizes how high he is.

The head following the poop dropping should carry most of the comedy. No additional dialogue is needed during the realization.

---

## FIRE_RESCUE

**Trigger:** The player reaches Wick.

**Animation:** Wick notices the player, still clinging nervously to the lantern.

**Wick:**
"AHSJFKH! YOU GET ME DOWN. DIGNIFIEDLY. NOW. GAH!!!"

The aboslutely terrified reaction should carry most of the comedy.

The climb begins after the seagull scene. This rescue line plays at the summit.

---

## FIRE_DESCENT

**Trigger:** The player picks Wick up for the descent.

**Animation:** Wick closes his eyes and holds on tightly.

**Wick:**
"Are we nearly down? Don't answer!"

No additional dialogue is necessary during the descent.

Gameplay should start now, where player heads down from top.

---

## FIRE_RESOLUTION

**Trigger:** The player reaches the festival grounds carrying Wick.

**Animation:** Wick opens his eyes, realizes he's safe, and visibly relaxes.

**Wick:**
"Ahhh. Glorious ground!"

**Trigger:** Wick presents the Candle.

**Animation:** Wick gives the player the Candle.

**Wick:**
"Thank you for helping. This candle is for you, it's a very important thing to us!"
