# Wick — Fire Salamander

## Overview

Wick is the guardian of the Fire world. He is a grown-up fantasy Fire Salamander with an extremely proud and cocky personality. He walks with 2 legs rather than 4, keeping his chest and head high.

He has always carried himself with his head held high and chest puffed out. His confidence is so excessive that he has never bothered looking down.

## Character

- **Name:** Wick
- **Species:** Fantasy Fire Salamander
- **Personality:** Cocky, proud, confident.
- **Role:** Festival guardian.
- **Reward:** Candle, given to the player as a thank-you gift for rescuing him.

## Story

Wick has always held his head high and looked upwards. He just became a grown salamander and conducted his first ever latern festival. He stood on a latern and flew up with it.

When the latern flies really high, a seagull flew by poops. Wick follows it with his eyes as it flies away, eventually looking down for the first time.

He suddenly realizes how high up he is and discovers that he is terrified of heights.

His perch is a ceremonial lantern gondola, which he normally steers down himself without looking. Now, however, he is too frightened to operate it.

The player climbs the lanterns to reach him, picks him up and carries him down by jumping between lanterns or dropping to the ground while he keeps his eyes shut.

After the rescue, Wick is relieved and comes away with a newfound humility.

## Character Arc

**Pride → Fear → Relief → Humility**

The humour comes from the contrast between Wick's ridiculously confident personality and his sudden, completely unexpected fear of heights.

## Visual Direction

- Grown-up Fire Salamander.
- Smooth, sculptural, organic creature design.
- Avoid low-poly or visibly assembled geometric body parts.
- A candle or flame on his head is a possible visual motif.
- His proud personality should be reflected in his posture and animations.

The muscular baby-like appearance previously discussed is an exaggerated comedic animation concept representing his pride and cockiness, not his actual character form.

## Production specification

**Essential.** Adult biped, 0.95 units tall with a broad 0.45-unit chest, narrower hips and a long curling counterweight tail. Shape head and torso as adjoining smooth swept sections, flatten the muzzle, bury the thighs and shoulders in the body. Place the eyes high on either side, keeping both pupils readable in three-quarter view. Charcoal skin (#352C38), saffron bands (#F2AA36), cream chin, amber iris. Carry one small wax nub and a teardrop flame on the crown; the gift is a separate stubby ivory Candle with a wax drip and dark wick. His title is festival guardian. Read pride in the raised muzzle and tall chest, then track the bird with pupils first, hinge the head down, tuck elbows, shut eyes and curl tail around himself. Fold him against the spirit while carried. At ground contact let his shoulders drop before he opens one eye and offers the Candle.

**Secondary polish.** A few asymmetric cheek spots, slow tail follow-through and a tiny flame lean. **Optional.** Stamped red band on the gift Candle. Use the [salamander colour blocking](references/wick/character.jpeg); preserve his upright stance.
