# Wood — Dialogues

## Presentation

- Use the standard retro-inspired dialogue box at the bottom of the screen.
- Character animations should play before their dialogue appears.
- Keep dialogue short and natural.
- Mallow speaks softly and hesitantly.
- Avoid excessive dialogue. Her expressions should communicate much of her personality.

## WOOD_INTRO

**Trigger:** Player approaches Mallow.

**Mallow:**
"U-um... hello!"

## WOOD_REQUEST

**Trigger:** Player interacts with Mallow.

**Mallow:**
"I was hoping to light this fire..."

"Everything's ready, but I don't have a flame."

"Oh! Is that a candle you're carrying?"

"Would you mind helping me?"

## WOOD_IGNITION

**Trigger:** Player successfully lights the fire.

**Mallow:**
"Yay! It worked!"

"So warm..."

## WOOD_REWARD

**Trigger:** Player interacts with Mallow after lighting the fire.

**Mallow:**
"Thank you so much for helping me!"

"Oh, by the way... what brings you here?"

**Player:**
"I'm looking for firewood. I need it to light a fire and brew some soup."

**Mallow:**
"Oh! I have some you can take. Please, take it!"

## Notes

- Keep a small pause before Mallow asks for help.
- She should appear slightly embarrassed when asking.
- Her reaction to the fire lighting should feel genuinely happy and relieved.
- The conversation about the player's objective happens only after the fire is lit.
- Firewood is the only reward.
