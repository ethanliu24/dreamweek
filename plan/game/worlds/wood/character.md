# Mallow — Wood Guardian

## Overview

Mallow is a shy mushroom spirit who lives inside the hollow of the enormous ancient tree in the Wood world. She loves cooking and takes her recipes very seriously, but she gets overwhelmed easily when things don't go according to plan.

Despite her timid nature, she is passionate about making a good soup. Cooking brings out a much more frantic and demanding side of her.

## Appearance

Mallow has a soft, rounded mushroom-like appearance. See `character_1.jpeg` in mallow references.

- **Head:** An oversized mushroom cap with a slightly drooping, uneven rim. Its surface has a few small pale spots.
- **Body:** A short, rounded, cream-coloured body resembling a mushroom stalk.
- **Face:** Large, warm brown eyes and a tiny mouth. Her expressions are simple but expressive.
- **Limbs:** Small arms and short legs.
- **Clothing:** A simple moss-green apron with a small pocket.
- **Colours:** Muted terracotta or dusty rose for the cap, warm cream for the body, and moss green for her apron.

Her overall appearance should feel soft, organic, and natural to the enchanted forest. Avoid making her look like a conventional human dressed in a mushroom costume; she should feel like a creature that naturally grew from the forest.

## Personality

Mallow is shy, polite, somewhat anxious, and passionate about cooking.

- Speaks softly and sometimes hesitates before speaking.
- Gets embarrassed easily, especially when someone watches her.
- Often avoids eye contact and looks down when talking.
- Takes her cooking and recipes very seriously.
- Has difficulty asking others for help.
- Becomes increasingly frantic when cooking doesn't go as expected.

Although she is timid, Mallow genuinely believes she is a capable cook. She simply has trouble handling pressure.

## Behaviour and Expressions

Mallow's behaviour should reflect her personality:

- **Idle:** Gently shifts her weight, looks around, and occasionally glances at the player.
- **Shy:** Looks down, brings her hands together, and slightly lowers her mushroom cap.
- **Embarrassed:** Avoids eye contact and hides part of her face beneath her cap.
- **Cooking:** Becomes focused and attentive, carefully watching the pot.
- **Panicking:** Starts moving around frantically, waving her arms and urgently pointing towards ingredients.
- **Relieved:** Relaxes, lets out a quiet sigh, and gives the player a small smile.

Her expressions should remain charming and slightly exaggerated, especially when her cooking anxiety takes over.

## Role in the Story

Mallow lives in the hollow of the enormous ancient tree. Her cooking station is set up in the clearing outside.

She is preparing a special soup but struggles to manage everything on her own. When the player arrives, she reluctantly asks for help gathering ingredients and preparing the soup.

As cooking progresses, her instructions become increasingly urgent. Her normally shy personality gives way to a frantic little chef who is determined to finish her recipe.

After the soup is prepared, Mallow rewards the player with **Firewood and a Healing Mushroom**.

## Character Arc

Mallow begins shy and hesitant to accept help. Through cooking with the player, she gradually becomes more comfortable and expressive.

Her main comedic contrast is between her timid everyday personality and her surprisingly intense behaviour when cooking is involved.

## Production specification

**Essential.** Mallow is 0.85 units tall. A wide drooping cap spans 0.8, almost twice her stalk width. Build the cap from a shallow curved dome plus a pale inset underside. Lower the front rim unevenly enough to hide one brow when she bows. Join a rounded cream stalk to tiny feet, sink the arm roots into its sides. Place warm brown oval eyes and a tiny mouth high on the stalk. Dusty rose cap #B76F68 with sparse pale spots, cream body #EBD6AF, moss-green apron #657954 with a stitched pocket following the belly arc. She clasps hands, tips the cap forward on request, opens arms at ignition and offers a tied log bundle and a smaller cream-stem healing mushroom. Her thank-you pose lifts the cap enough to show both eyes.

**Secondary polish.** Indicate underside gills with vertex colour stripes and let the cap lag on the relieved nod. **Optional.** One pocket patch. Use the [cap and stalk reference](references/mallow/character_1.jpeg).
