# Wood — Story

## Overview

The player enters an enchanted forest clearing and meets Mallow, a shy mushroom spirit.

Mallow has prepared a fire pit outside her home but has no way to light it. The player uses Wick's Candle to help her start the fire.

Mallow thanks the player and gives them Firewood.

**Target duration:** 30–60 seconds.

## Story Scenes

### Scene 1 — Arrival

**Scene ID:** `WOOD_INTRO`

**Presentation:** Brief establishing shot followed by player control.

The player arrives in the forest clearing. The enormous ancient tree stands in the background, with Mallow's home inside its trunk.

Mallow is standing beside an unlit fire pit.

**Camera:**
- Begin with a wide shot establishing the ancient tree and clearing.
- Slowly move the camera towards Mallow and the fire pit.
- Return control to the player.

**Character animation:**
- Mallow idles beside the fire pit.
- She notices the player approaching and looks towards them.

**Player control:** Disabled during the opening shot, then enabled.

### Scene 2 — Mallow's Request

**Scene ID:** `WOOD_REQUEST`

**Presentation:** Short character interaction.

Mallow greets the player. She explains that she has prepared some firewood but cannot light the fire because she doesn't have a flame.

She notices the Candle the player carries and asks whether they could help.

**Camera:**
- Frame Mallow and the fire pit together.
- Briefly focus on the prepared firewood when she mentions it.
- Return to a medium shot of Mallow.

**Character animation:**
- Mallow initially looks down while speaking.
- She gestures towards the fire pit.
- When she notices the Candle, she looks up with a little hope.

**Player control:** Enabled after the dialogue. The player can approach the fire pit and interact.

### Scene 3 — Light the Fire

**Scene ID:** `WOOD_IGNITION`

**Presentation:** Gameplay interaction followed by a short animation.

The player uses Wick's Candle on the prepared firewood.

The fire lights successfully.

**Camera:**
- Focus on the fire pit during the interaction.
- Briefly emphasize the fire catching.
- Show Mallow's reaction.

**Character animation:**
- The fire transitions from unlit to lit.
- Mallow reacts with surprise and happiness.
- She relaxes once the fire is burning.

**Player control:** Disabled briefly during the lighting animation, then restored.

### Scene 4 — Reward

**Scene ID:** `WOOD_REWARD`

**Presentation:** Short character interaction.

Mallow thanks the player for helping her.

She gives the player a bundle of Firewood.

**Camera:**
- Medium shot of Mallow and the player.
- Briefly show the rewards as they are given.

**Character animation:**
- Mallow smiles and gestures towards her woodpile.
- She shyly offers the rewards.

**Player control:** Disabled during the interaction. Restored after the rewards are collected.

### Scene 5 — Return

**Scene ID:** `WOOD_EXIT`

**Presentation:** Transition.

The player leaves the dream and returns to the overworld.

**Camera:** Fade to black, then transition to the overworld.

**Player control:** Disabled during the transition.

## Storyboard Summary

1. Establish the ancient tree and forest clearing.
2. Reveal Mallow beside an unlit fire pit.
3. Mallow asks the player for help lighting the fire.
4. The player uses Wick's Candle.
5. The fire lights and Mallow reacts happily.
6. Mallow gives the player Firewood.
7. Fade back to the overworld.

## Notes

- Keep the story short and straightforward.
- Mallow's shy personality should carry the interaction.
- The fire lighting is the only gameplay interaction.
- Do not introduce a cooking minigame, gathering quest, or additional puzzle.
- The firewood in the fire pit is separate from the bundle of Firewood given as a reward.
