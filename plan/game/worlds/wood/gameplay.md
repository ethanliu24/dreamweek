# Wood — Gameplay

## Overview

A short character encounter in which the player helps Mallow light a prepared fire pit using Wick's Candle.

The gameplay is intentionally minimal. The focus is on the interaction with Mallow and the atmosphere of the forest.

**Target duration:** 30–60 seconds.

## Objective

Help Mallow light the fire outside her home.

## Gameplay Flow

### 1. Meet Mallow

- The player arrives in the forest clearing.
- Mallow is standing beside an unlit fire pit.
- The player talks to her and learns that she has prepared firewood but cannot light it.

### 2. Light the Fire

- The player approaches the fire pit.
- The player uses the Candle obtained from the Fire world.
- The fire pit changes from unlit to lit.

This is the only gameplay interaction in the world.

### 3. Receive Rewards

- Mallow thanks the player.
- The player receives:
  - Firewood
  - Healing Mushroom
- The world is completed.

## Mechanics and Rules

- The firewood is already arranged in the fire pit.
- The player does not need to gather or arrange anything.
- The player uses the existing Candle item to light the fire.
- Lighting the fire is a single successful interaction.
- There are no timers, failure states, or additional challenges.

## Rewards

**Firewood**

Used later in the overworld as fuel for the soup preparation.

**Healing Mushroom**

A healing item that can be used by the player.

## Overworld Integration

After returning to the overworld:

- Add Firewood to the player's collected rewards.
- Add the Healing Mushroom to the player's collected rewards.
- The player can later place the Firewood beneath the soup pot and use the Candle to light it.

The fire pit inside the Wood dream is separate from the soup-cooking setup in the overworld.

## Constraints

- Keep the encounter short, ideally 30–60 seconds.
- No cooking minigame.
- No exploration or ingredient collection.
- No fire-building or wood-arranging puzzle.
- No timing or rhythm mechanics.
- No elaborate fire simulation.
- Use simple character reactions and a clear fire-lighting effect.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The player can meet and interact with Mallow.
- Mallow explains that she needs help lighting her fire.
- The player can use Wick's Candle on the prepared firewood.
- The fire changes from unlit to lit.
- Mallow reacts positively.
- The player receives Firewood and a Healing Mushroom.
- The player can return to the overworld with both rewards.
