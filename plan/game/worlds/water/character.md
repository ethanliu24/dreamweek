# Teal — Lifeguard Seal

## 1. Overview

Teal is the guardian of the Water world and works as the beach lifeguard.

He is responsible for closing the beach after someone pees in the ocean. Ironically, he is the culprit himself.

Teal initially behaves like an ordinary, professional lifeguard. As the player investigates and uncovers evidence, he becomes increasingly nervous until eventually confessing.

## 2. Character Identity

- **Name:** Teal
- **Species:** Seal
- **Role:** Beach lifeguard
- **Personality:** Initially calm and professional, but increasingly nervous and defensive as the investigation progresses.
- **Secret:** He is the person responsible for peeing in the ocean.

## 3. Appearance and Visual Direction

### Overall Shape
- Small, rounded, plump seal.
- Smooth, cohesive body shape.
- Cream-white or slightly off-white colouring.
- Small flippers.
- Rounded muzzle.

### Face and Accessories
- Oversized dark sunglasses as his defining visual feature.
- Simple, expressive facial features.
- A whistle hanging around his neck.
- Optional simple red or orange lifeguard accessories.

### Modeling Requirements
- Smooth, organic sculptural design.
- Avoid visibly assembled geometric body parts.
- Keep the overall silhouette simple and recognizable.
- Use the sunglasses and whistle as his main identifying features.

The visual direction takes inspiration from the rounded cartoon seal design in *A Hat in Time: Seal the Deal*, while maintaining an original character design.

## 4. Personality and Behaviour

Teal's personality is primarily communicated through his changing behaviour during the investigation.

### Initially — Professional
- Relaxed and composed.
- Takes his lifeguard responsibilities seriously.
- Acts like an ordinary, professional lifeguard.

### During Investigation — Increasingly Nervous
- Becomes visibly uncomfortable as evidence accumulates.
- Starts acting defensive.
- Fidgets more frequently.
- Adjusts his sunglasses nervously.
- Avoids eye contact.
- Struggles to maintain his professional composure.

### Confession — Embarrassed
- Finally admits that he was responsible.
- Appears embarrassed and relieved to have confessed.
- The situation resolves without portraying him as malicious.

## 5. Animation Direction

Keep animations simple and expressive.

Possible animations:
- Relaxed idle animation.
- Adjusting his sunglasses.
- Touching or checking his whistle.
- Nervous fidgeting.
- Looking away or avoiding eye contact.
- Awkward reactions when presented with evidence.
- An embarrassed expression during confession.

The main goal is to gradually communicate his nervousness through increasingly obvious body language.

## Production specification

**Essential.** Seated height 0.9, belly width 0.75. Sweep one tapered seal body from twin tail flukes to a rounded brow, inset two muzzle pads into a cream face (#F2E4CB). Flippers emerge midway down the sides. Pin a charcoal nose above a short mouth line. Fit oversized rounded smoke-blue lenses with a dark bridge across almost the full head width. Add a muted red lanyard; a missing pendant during the investigation lets the coral whistle be the clue. Start upright on the chair, hinge one flipper up to adjust glasses. For each piece of evidence increase the pause before answering, tip his body away and lower the lenses slightly. On confession he slumps and lets his flippers fall. Keep the seated silhouette readable from underwater return path.

**Secondary polish.** A slim sky reflection moves across the lenses; tiny whisker dimples catch side light. **Optional.** A red stripe on the chair cushion. Study [seated scale and pose](references/teal/character_main_reference_on_lifeguard_seat.jpeg).
