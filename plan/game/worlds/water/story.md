# Water — Story

## 1. Overview

The player arrives at a sunny beach carrying the empty pot from Earth.

After filling the pot with water from a nearby beach faucet, a shell siren sounds. The beach is suddenly placed under lockdown because someone has peed in the ocean.

The lifeguard, Teal, announces that nobody is allowed to leave until the culprit is found.

The player must investigate the incident. The investigation leads underwater, where they discover evidence implicating Teal himself.

Teal initially maintains his innocence but becomes increasingly nervous as the player uncovers evidence. Eventually, he confesses and the beach reopens.

The player leaves with the filled pot.

## 2. Story Structure

The experience consists of seven scenes:

1. **Arrival:** Introduce the beach and allow the player to collect water.
2. **Lockdown:** Teal announces the incident and closes the beach.
3. **Underwater Access:** A sleepy giant clam provides a protective bubble.
4. **First Lead:** A crab witness provides the first clue.
5. **The Missing Whistle:** The player finds Teal's whistle, and Teal claims it was stolen.
6. **Jellyfish Garden:** Another witness provides the final piece of evidence.
7. **Confrontation and Resolution:** Teal confesses, the beach reopens, and the player leaves.

All underwater investigation takes place in one compact environment. The player does not enter separate levels or subscenes.

## 3. Narrative Sequence

### Scene 1 — Arrival at the Beach

**Type:** Environment introduction and gameplay.

**Purpose:** Establish the beach and introduce the player's immediate objective.

- The player arrives at a sunny beach.
- Beachgoers are relaxing and enjoying the ocean.
- Teal is stationed at his lifeguard stand.
- The player discovers a small faucet near the beach.
- The player interacts with the faucet to fill the pot carried from Earth.
- Once the water is collected, a shell siren suddenly sounds.

**Camera direction:**
- Begin with a wide establishing shot of the beach and ocean.
- Briefly show the lifeguard stand and surrounding beachgoers.
- Return to the normal gameplay camera.
- Keep the faucet visible and recognizable.

**Player control:** Enabled throughout exploration and water collection.

### Scene 2 — Beach Lockdown

**Type:** Scripted announcement and environmental change.

**Purpose:** Introduce the investigation.

- The shell siren sounds across the beach.
- Teal gets everyone's attention.
- He announces that someone has peed in the ocean.
- The beach is immediately closed until the culprit is identified.
- A barrier and BEACH CLOSED sign appear across the beach exit.
- Teal takes responsibility for investigating the incident.

**Animation:**
- Teal reacts to the siren.
- He gets into a more serious lifeguard posture.
- He makes the announcement.
- Beachgoers react with confusion and concern.
- The lockdown barrier is raised.

**Camera direction:**
- Use a medium shot of Teal as the siren sounds.
- Briefly frame him addressing the beach.
- Show the barrier being placed across the exit.
- Return to the gameplay camera once the announcement is over.

**Player control:** Temporarily disabled during the announcement, then restored.

**Dialogue:** See `dialogs.md` → `WATER_LOCKDOWN`.

### Scene 3 — Underwater Access

**Type:** Scripted interaction and transition into exploration.

**Purpose:** Introduce the protective bubble and allow underwater investigation.

- The player discovers a giant sleepy clam near the lifeguard station.
- Interacting with the clam causes it to wake up slightly.
- The clam blows a large protective bubble around the player.
- The bubble allows the player to explore underwater without directly touching the water.
- The clam immediately goes back to sleep.

The protective bubble stays in the Water dream.

**Animation:**
- The clam slowly opens its shell.
- It blows a large bubble around the player.
- The player becomes enclosed in the bubble.
- The clam closes its shell and returns to sleep.

**Camera direction:**
- Use a medium shot to introduce the clam.
- Briefly focus on the bubble forming around the player.
- Transition into the underwater environment as the player enters the ocean.

**Player control:** Temporarily paused during the bubble interaction, then restored.

The underwater environment is one continuous, compact area containing the investigation locations.

**Dialogue:** See `dialogs.md` → `WATER_CLAM`.

### Scene 4 — First Lead: The Crab

**Type:** Exploration and character interaction.

**Purpose:** Introduce the first piece of evidence.

- While exploring the coral area, the player encounters a crab witness.
- The crab mentions seeing someone wearing a whistle enter the water.
- This suggests that the culprit may be someone associated with the beach staff.
- The player continues investigating.

**Animation:**
- The crab notices the player.
- It gestures or points towards where it saw the swimmer.
- Keep its animation simple and expressive.

**Camera direction:**
- Use the normal underwater gameplay camera during exploration.
- Frame the crab in a medium shot during the interaction.
- Return to gameplay after the conversation.

**Player control:** Enabled during exploration and restored after the interaction.

**Dialogue:** See `dialogs.md` → `WATER_CRAB`.
