# The Void — Scenery

## Overview

A mysterious cosmic realm surrounded by endless darkness and dominated by an enormous celestial body.

The Void is the setting for the final Sun and Moon encounters. Both encounters take place in the same continuous environment.

The visual direction should feel vast, mysterious, and slightly ominous, with a strong cosmic aesthetic.

The environment should be visually striking without being cluttered.

## Celestial Background

The main visual landmark is a gigantic purple gas planet.

- Occupies a large portion of the visible background.
- Deep purple surface with subtle variations in tone.
- Soft swirling patterns and atmospheric details.
- A luminous violet and magenta edge around parts of the planet.
- Gentle cosmic glow illuminating the surrounding darkness.

The celestial body should feel enormous and distant, making the arena appear tiny in comparison.

It is a separate celestial object, not the missing Moon.

## Floating Arena

A large white platform floats alone in the cosmic void.

- **Shape:** Broad, slightly irregular circular platform.
- **Surface:** Smooth white stone with a porcelain-like appearance.
- **Edges:** Soft, rounded, sculptural edges.
- **Underside:** Subtle lavender or violet glow.
- **Structure:** Suspended freely in space, with no visible supports.
- **Details:** Very subtle engraved patterns or glowing lines.

The arena should feel almost otherworldly, like an ancient celestial platform.

Keep the surface clean and open, with enough space for the encounters.

The same arena is used for both the Sun encounter and Moon boss fight.

## Floating Details

Keep floating objects extremely limited.

- A few small rock fragments near the distant edges.
- Occasional distant debris.
- Sparse glowing particles.

These details should reinforce the feeling of being in space without distracting from the arena or celestial background.

Avoid additional platforms, floating islands, or elaborate structures.

## Space and Atmosphere

The surrounding environment is almost entirely black.

- Deep black space.
- Very subtle distant stars.
- Dark purple cosmic haze.
- Soft violet and magenta atmospheric gradients.
- Sparse glowing particles.

The environment should feel enormous and mostly empty.

## Colour Palette

- **Base:** Black and near-black.
- **Primary:** Deep cosmic purple and violet.
- **Secondary:** Magenta and lavender.
- **Arena:** White and very pale lavender.
- **Highlights:** Brighter purple and subtle pink.

Black and deep purple should dominate the background, while the white arena provides a strong visual contrast.

## Lighting

The giant celestial object provides the main atmospheric illumination.

- Subtle purple ambient lighting.
- Soft violet reflections on the arena.
- Gentle atmospheric glow around the celestial body.
- A faint glow underneath the floating platform.

Important gameplay objects and characters should remain clearly visible.

## Visual Priorities

1. The enormous purple celestial body.
2. The white floating arena.
3. The dark cosmic atmosphere.
4. Sparse floating fragments and particles.

## Scope and Constraints

- One continuous environment for the Sun and Moon encounters.
- One white floating arena.
- One enormous purple celestial background object.
- Very limited floating debris and decorative objects.
- No elaborate space structures or additional explorable areas.
- Avoid excessive visual clutter.
- Prioritize the contrast between the white arena and dark cosmic surroundings.

## Production specification

**Essential.** One white disc roughly 18 units across, gently irregular outer radius, rolled lip and narrow lavender underside. Use porcelain #E4DEEF with low-contrast radial engravings. A dark foot shadow anchors the pale spirit. At arrival look across the arena toward the Wizard. Crop a huge gas sphere behind his shoulder so its curved limb fills about half the sky. Use #24133F bands, a narrow magenta sunward rim and near-black space. Keep the real Moon visible only inside the Wizard. At most three small distant fragments. Pull the camera back during combat to keep charge paths and purple shock rings readable. Identify Sun Essence with a golden-white orb and fast gold sparkles, and Moon Essence with a silver-lavender orb and slower violet sparkles. Bob them independently at reachable height.

Finisher on Q: hold a low angle on the spirit drawing the Sword for 0.35 s, gather a thin gold ring at its grip, arc the blade across the frame with a single gold ribbon over 0.3 s, then cut wide. A violet ring dissipates at the Wizard's feet and he folds unconscious over 0.65 s. Keep his belly Moon illuminated, clear the ribbon before the Moon Essence rises. Use one fixed wide shot and a short arc in reduced motion.
