# The Grand Wizard — Frog Wizard

## Overview

The Grand Wizard is a powerful frog wizard who despises Mondays.

After discovering a forbidden technique in an ancient historical book, he swallows the Moon, believing it will eliminate Monday from the calendar.

His actions cause the calendar to collapse and disrupt the seven interconnected worlds.

He is the main antagonist of Dreamweek and the final boss in The Void.

## Appearance

The Grand Wizard is an oversized, chubby frog wizard.

- **Size:** Approximately twice the height of the protagonist.
- **Body:** Large, rounded frog body with smooth green skin.
- **Belly:** An enormous, round belly that has become particularly large after swallowing the Moon.
- **Moon:** The actual Moon is visibly glowing inside his belly. Its pale white and lavender light can be seen through the slightly translucent magical surface.
- **Eyes:** Two large, protruding frog eyes positioned very far apart on top of his head. Each eye looks in a different direction, giving him a comically unfocused appearance.
- **Mouth:** A wide frog mouth with expressive features.
- **Arms:** Short, rounded frog arms with webbed fingers.
- **Legs:** Thick, short frog legs capable of supporting his oversized body.
- **Clothing:** A traditional purple witch outfit with an oversized, tall, slightly floppy witch hat.
- **Details:** Subtle magical markings and glowing purple accents on his clothing.

His enormous belly and widely separated eyes should be his most recognizable features.

The Moon's glow should remain visible even when he is not using magic.

## Colour Palette

- **Skin:** Rich green with slightly lighter green tones around the belly and face.
- **Belly:** Pale green with a subtle magical translucency.
- **Clothing:** Deep purple and violet.
- **Witch hat:** Dark purple, matching his outfit.
- **Magical effects:** Bright violet, purple, and magenta.
- **Moon:** Pale white with soft lavender highlights.

His appearance should fit the stylized, dreamy aesthetic of Dreamweek. Use smooth organic shapes and exaggerated proportions rather than realistic frog anatomy.

## Personality

The Grand Wizard is dramatic, self-important, and childish.

- Absolutely despises Mondays.
- Takes himself very seriously.
- Believes he is an exceptionally powerful and intelligent wizard.
- Enjoys dramatic gestures and grand declarations.
- Gets angry when things don't go according to his plans.
- Is overly confident in his magical abilities.
- Becomes increasingly frustrated when his minions fail.

The comedy comes from the contrast between his grand title and his ridiculous hatred of one particular day of the week.

## Behaviour and Expressions

- **Idle:** Stands proudly, occasionally moving his independently-looking eyes and adjusting his witch hat.
- **Angry:** Puffs up his cheeks, widens his eyes, and gestures aggressively.
- **Casting:** Raises his arms and gathers glowing purple magical energy.
- **Surprised:** Opens his mouth and widens his eyes.
- **Exhausted:** Becomes visibly sluggish and struggles to move his enormous belly.
- **Defeated:** Collapses onto his belly and becomes unconscious.

## Combat Moveset

The Grand Wizard is the final boss in The Void. His combat consists of three simple attacks.

### 1. Belly Bounce

The Grand Wizard uses his enormous belly and weight to attack the player.

**Animation sequence:**
1. Briefly crouches and compresses his body.
2. Jumps towards the player's position.
3. Lands heavily on the arena.
4. Produces a circular purple shockwave.
5. Returns to his normal stance.

The attack should feel exaggerated and slightly ridiculous.

### 2. Magic Burst

The Grand Wizard creates and launches a magical projectile.

**Animation sequence:**
1. Raises one hand.
2. A glowing purple orb forms above his hand.
3. Launches the orb towards the player's position.
4. Briefly recovers before his next action.

Only one projectile is launched at a time.

### 3. Tongue Swipe

The Grand Wizard uses his long frog tongue to attack.

**Animation sequence:**
1. Opens his mouth.
2. Extends his tongue directly forward.
3. Holds it extended briefly.
4. Retracts it.
5. Returns to his normal stance.

The attack should have a clear direction and predictable movement.

## Abilities

### Magical Summoning

The Grand Wizard can summon magical minions to fight on his behalf.

He summons small floating magical cubes with dark surfaces and glowing purple markings.

These minions rotate continuously and attack by charging towards the player.

The summoning ability is used before the boss encounter, during the first stage of The Void.

### Moon Absorption

The Grand Wizard has swallowed the actual Moon.

- The Moon remains visible inside his enormous belly.
- It produces a soft white and lavender glow.
- Its magical presence is connected to his ability to disrupt the calendar.
- The Moon is not released when he is defeated.

The actual Moon is only released when the Grand Wizard eats the completed magical soup in the Overworld.

## Combat Behaviour

- Uses Belly Bounce, Magic Burst, and Tongue Swipe throughout the boss encounter.
- Each attack should have a recognizable animation before it happens.
- Attacks apply a small amount of knockback to the player when they connect.
- His movements should feel slightly heavy because of his enormous belly.
- Keep attack animations exaggerated but relatively simple.
- He eventually becomes overwhelmed by the player's attacks.

The player ultimately defeats him using the newly unlocked ultimate ability.

## Defeat

After the player's ultimate finishes the encounter, the Grand Wizard collapses onto the arena and becomes unconscious.

He is not permanently dead.

A glowing Moon Essence emerges from his magic and can be collected by the player.

The actual Moon remains inside his belly.

The player can then approach him and drag him back to the Overworld for the final soup sequence.

## Character Direction

The Grand Wizard should be a memorable, comedic antagonist.

His enormous belly, widely separated eyes looking in different directions, oversized witch hat, and exaggerated behaviour should make him visually distinct.

Keep his design cohesive and relatively simple, using smooth organic shapes and a limited number of recognizable features.

His combat animations should communicate his attacks clearly without requiring complicated character animation.

## Production specification

**Essential.** Body and head together stand roughly twice the spirit's height; floppy hat extends another 0.45 units. A forward-heavy pear body carries a round belly over wide short haunches; merge the broad snout contour into cheeks, place eye turrets far apart at the top corners and aim their pupils in different directions. Build the mouth from a curved recessed seam with a hinged lower lip. Bury stubby arm roots, spread webbed fingertips and bulb the toes. Green skin #719459, light belly #B6C68C. Render the actual Moon as a pale disc with faint crater markings beneath a small belly window or layered tinted patch. Keep it visible during idle, attacks and capture. A cutaway deep-violet robe and bent ring-stack cone hat leave the belly exposed.

Anticipate belly bounce by widening knees and squashing, then land into a flat purple shock ring. Lift one hand before the single orb launch. Open mouth wide before a straight pink tongue unrolls and retracts. On defeat pivot forward around the hips, close mismatched eyes and settle onto the belly. Reuse the identical model and unconscious pose at the hub, then swell cheeks on feeding.

**Secondary polish.** Hat tip and cuffs settle a beat behind the body, crescent collar markings frame the belly. **Optional.** One stitched hat patch. Use [eye spacing and costume mass](references/grand_wizard/frog_costume_and_eyes.jpeg).
