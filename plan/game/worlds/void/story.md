# The Void — Story

## Overview

The Void is the setting for the final encounter of Dreamweek.

The player arrives at a white floating arena surrounded by darkness, with an enormous purple celestial body dominating the background.

The Frog Wizard is waiting for the player. After confronting them, he summons magical minions to stop them.

Once the minions are defeated, the Frog Wizard enters the arena himself. The encounter ends with the player using their ultimate ability to defeat him.

The player collects both magical essences and brings the unconscious Frog Wizard back to the Overworld.

Sun and Moon take place in the same continuous environment, without returning to the Overworld between encounters.

## Scene 1 — Arrival

**Scene ID:** `VOID_INTRO`

**Presentation:** Brief establishing shot followed by character interaction.

The player arrives on the white floating arena. The enormous purple celestial body is visible in the background.

The Frog Wizard is waiting on the opposite side of the arena. He notices the player and confronts them.

He eventually summons a group of magical cube minions to attack.

**Camera:**
- Begin with a wide shot establishing the white arena and enormous celestial background.
- Reveal the Frog Wizard on the opposite side.
- Briefly frame the player and Frog Wizard facing each other.

**Character animation:**
- Frog Wizard stands confidently.
- He notices the player and gestures towards them.
- He performs a magical summoning gesture.
- Dark purple magical energy gathers around him.
- The cube minions materialize in the arena.

**Player control:** Disabled during the initial presentation and dialogue. Enabled when the encounter begins.

**Dialogue reference:** `VOID_INTRO`

## Scene 2 — Sun: Minion Encounter

**Scene ID:** `VOID_MINIONS`

**Presentation:** Combat gameplay.

The Frog Wizard's magical cube minions attack the player.

The player defeats the minions. Once the arena is clear, the Frog Wizard reacts to their defeat.

A glowing golden-white orb appears in the arena. This is the Sun Essence.

The player collects the essence, obtaining the first of the two magical essences needed to restore the calendar.

**Camera:**
- Remain in the normal gameplay perspective during the encounter.
- After the final minion is defeated, briefly emphasize the empty arena.
- Focus on the appearance of the Sun Essence.

**Character animation:**
- Cube minions float and rotate around the arena.
- Their magical markings glow as they attack.
- Defeated minions disappear in a brief magical effect.
- Frog Wizard reacts when his minions are defeated.
- Sun Essence appears with a soft golden glow.

**Player control:** Enabled during the encounter and collection. Temporarily restricted during the transition to the next sequence.

**Dialogue reference:** `VOID_AFTER_MINIONS`

## Scene 3 — Moon: Frog Wizard Encounter

**Scene ID:** `VOID_BOSS`

**Presentation:** Boss encounter.

After his minions are defeated, the Frog Wizard decides to fight the player himself.

He enters the arena and begins attacking the player using his magic and physical abilities.

The player fights back. As the encounter progresses, the Frog Wizard becomes increasingly overwhelmed.

Eventually, the player's ultimate ability becomes available.

### Ultimate Finisher

The player activates the ultimate ability.

**Animation and visual design:** To be designed later.

The ultimate should have a dramatic, cinematic presentation inspired by action-game and gacha-game finishing moves.

The sequence ends with a powerful final attack against the Frog Wizard.

He collapses onto the arena, defeated and unconscious.

**Camera:**
- Keep the normal gameplay camera during the encounter.
- During the ultimate, transition into a more cinematic presentation.
- Focus on the final impact and the Frog Wizard's defeat.
- Return to the normal arena view afterwards.

**Character animation:**
- Frog Wizard uses his planned attack animations.
- His reactions become more exaggerated as the encounter progresses.
- During the ultimate, he is caught in the finishing sequence.
- After the final impact, he collapses onto the arena.

**Player control:** Enabled during combat. Disabled during the ultimate sequence, then restored after the Frog Wizard is defeated.

**Dialogue reference:** `VOID_BOSS_END`

## Scene 4 — The Two Essences

**Scene ID:** `VOID_ESSENCE`

**Presentation:** Short collection sequence.

With the Frog Wizard unconscious, a second glowing orb emerges from his magic.

This is the Moon Essence, represented by a silver-white orb with purple and lavender light.

The player collects it.

The player now has both Sun Essence and Moon Essence.

The actual Moon is still inside the Frog Wizard. The Moon Essence is not the Moon itself.

**Camera:**
- Briefly focus on the Moon Essence as it appears.
- Return to a view of the unconscious Frog Wizard and player.
- Keep the sequence short.

**Character animation:**
- The Moon Essence gently floats above the arena.
- The unconscious Frog Wizard remains motionless.
- The protagonist collects the essence.

## Scene 5 — Bringing Back the Frog Wizard

**Scene ID:** `VOID_CAPTURE`

**Presentation:** Short interaction followed by transition.

The Frog Wizard remains unconscious on the white arena.

The player approaches him and brings him back to the Overworld.

**Camera:**
- Show the unconscious Frog Wizard.
- Briefly frame the protagonist beside him.
- Transition back to the normal camera presentation.

**Character animation:**
- The protagonist grabs the Frog Wizard.
- The Frog Wizard remains unconscious.

**Player control:** Available during the approach and interaction. Disabled during the return transition.

**Dialogue reference:** None.

## Scene 6 — Return to the Overworld

**Scene ID:** `VOID_EXIT`

**Presentation:** Transition.

The player and unconscious Frog Wizard return to the Overworld.

The same circular black transition used by the other dream worlds closes over the screen and opens again to reveal the Overworld.

The protagonist has returned with both essences and the defeated Frog Wizard.

This leads directly into the final soup preparation and conclusion documented in the Overworld story.

**Camera:** Use the established circular transition.

**Player control:** Disabled during the transition.

## Storyboard Summary

1. Establish the white floating arena and enormous purple celestial background.
2. Reveal the Frog Wizard waiting for the player.
3. Frog Wizard confronts the player and summons magical cube minions.
4. Player defeats the minions.
5. Sun Essence appears and is collected.
6. Frog Wizard enters the arena himself.
7. Player fights the Frog Wizard.
8. Player activates the ultimate ability.
9. The ultimate finishes the Frog Wizard, who collapses unconscious.
10. Moon Essence emerges from his magic and is collected.
11. Player approaches and brings back the unconscious Frog Wizard.
12. Circular transition returns the player to the Overworld.

## Notes

- Sun and Moon are one continuous encounter in the same environment.
- Do not return to the Overworld between the two encounters.
- Use the same white floating arena for both encounters.
- Keep the ultimate's exact animation and visual design open for later.
- Sun Essence and Moon Essence are visually distinct glowing orbs.
- The actual Moon remains inside the Frog Wizard.
- The Frog Wizard is unconscious, not permanently dead.
- Avoid additional cutscenes, characters, or unnecessary story sequences.
- Combat rules and enemy mechanics belong in `gameplay.md`.
- Actual dialogue belongs in `dialogs.md`.
