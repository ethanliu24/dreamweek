# The Void — Dialogues

## Presentation

- Use the standard retro-inspired dialogue box at the bottom of the screen.
- Character animations should play before dialogue appears.
- Keep exchanges short and expressive.
- The Frog Wizard is dramatic, arrogant, and confident in his magical abilities.
- Avoid unnecessary dialogue during combat.

## VOID_INTRO

**Trigger:** Player arrives in the Void and encounters the Frog Wizard.

**Frog Wizard:**

"So... you've come all this way to stop me? For whatever it's called? BFCM?"

"What does that stand for? Bacon Frice Chicken Mayo?"

"Sounds amazing..."

"BUT..."

"I will NEVER bring back Monday!"

"Now, deal with my minions!"

## VOID_AFTER_MINIONS

**Trigger:** Player defeats all magical cube minions.

**Frog Wizard:**

"WHAT?! You defeated all of them?!"

"Fine! I'll stop you myself!"

## VOID_BOSS_END

**Trigger:** Player defeats the Frog Wizard using the ultimate ability.

**Presentation:** The Frog Wizard collapses unconscious after the ultimate.

**Protagonist (internal monologue):**

"Finally... Now I can buy my washing machine on sale."

## Notes

- The Frog Wizard should sound overly confident before combat.
- His reaction after the minion wave should be exaggerated and comedic.
- Keep the transition into the boss fight immediate.
- The Frog Wizard is unconscious after the ultimate and does not speak during the capture sequence.
- No additional dialogue is needed when collecting the Sun or Moon Essence.
